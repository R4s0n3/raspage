moodpage · privacy

Last updated 21 October 2026

moodpage is a private diary whose pages are weather. It is built so that your words stay yours: only weather is ever shared, and only with people you choose.

What stays on your phone

Your diary entries and your past days are stored only on your phone. They are not uploaded, backed up, or synced by moodpage.

Turning an entry into weather

When you choose “Make the weather”, the entry is sent once, over an encrypted connection, to the moodpage server, which passes it to an AI model through OpenRouter to be translated into weather. The request is only routed to model providers that do not keep or train on what they are sent. The moodpage server does not store or log the entry; it returns the weather and forgets the words.

Your account

To share weather with friends, the app creates an account for you. There is no email, phone number, or password. The server keeps only:

  • a one-way fingerprint (hash) of the secret key the app keeps in your phone's keychain;
  • the name you choose for your friends to see;
  • who your friends are, and the invites you have made (an invite expires after seven days, or when it is used);
  • your latest weather and its date (never the entry it came from).

Nobody can search for you. Only your friends can see your name and your latest weather.

Drawing together

When you and a friend are in the same water, the touches you draw are relayed between your phones as positions on the screen. They are not stored.

What is not collected

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, and no sale or sharing of data with anyone. To prevent abuse, the server counts requests per account or network address for an hour at a time; only the counts are kept, and they are then forgotten.

Deleting your data

In the app, Friends → “Delete my account” removes your account, name, friendships, invites, and shared weather from the server immediately. Your diary stays on your phone until you delete the app.
