moodpage · privacy

Last updated 21 October 2026

moodpage is a private diary whose pages are weather. It is built so that your words stay yours: only weather is ever shared, and only with people you choose. This page explains exactly what happens with your data.

Who is responsible

What stays on your phone

Your diary entries and your past days are stored only on your phone. They are not uploaded, backed up, or synced by moodpage. Deleting the app deletes them.

Turning an entry into weather

When you choose “Make the weather”, the entry is sent once, over an encrypted connection, to the moodpage server. The server passes it to an AI model through OpenRouter (OpenRouter, Inc., United States) to be translated into weather, and returns only the weather. Requests are only routed to model providers that do not store or train on what they are sent. The moodpage server does not store or log the entry.

Because a diary can say something about your health or private life, we only send an entry when you ask for it, by choosing “Make the weather” (Art. 9(2)(a) and Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can stop at any time by not using it; the rest of the app keeps working. OpenRouter and the model providers may process the entry in the United States; the transfer relies on the safeguards these providers offer for international transfers.

Your account

To share weather with friends, the app creates an account for you. There is no email, phone number, or password. The server keeps only:

  • a one-way fingerprint (hash) of the secret key the app keeps in your phone's keychain;
  • the name you choose for your friends to see;
  • who your friends are, and the invites you have made (an invite expires after seven days, or when it is used);
  • your latest weather and its date (never the entry it came from);
  • touches you leave in a friend's water while they are away (“stones”), until your friend's app collects them, at most seven days.

Nobody can search for you. Only your friends can see your name and your latest weather. This is needed to provide the friends features you choose to use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Account data is kept until you delete your account.

Drawing together

When you and a friend are in the same water, the touches you draw are relayed between your phones as positions on the screen, and are not stored.

Security and abuse prevention

To protect the service, the server counts requests per account or network address for one hour at a time; only the counts are kept, then forgotten. The server's hosting may keep short-lived technical access logs (such as IP address, time and requested address) to keep the service secure. This is based on our legitimate interest in a secure, working service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

What is not collected

No advertising, no analytics, no cookies, no tracking across apps or websites, and no sale of data. The app and this website are not directed at children under 16.

Deleting your data

In the app, Friends → “Delete my account” removes your account, name, friendships, invites, stones and shared weather from the server immediately. Your diary stays on your phone until you delete the app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive it in a portable form, and to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. Most of this you can do in the app directly; otherwise contact us at the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

Changes
