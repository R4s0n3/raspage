## Notes

This is the foundation entry, not a diary prompt. The page is deliberately
wordless and quiet enough to become a place rather than a statement.

The visual language is a single weather system: light, cloud, and dust in a deep
field. Future entries should bend this system — temperature, weight, tempo,
density — rather than replace it. The first real diary entry will turn
*Threshold* into a specific day.

The journal in this repository is an atmospheric log. The diary itself remains
private.
